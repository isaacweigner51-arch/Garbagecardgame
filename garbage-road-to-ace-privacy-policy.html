Privacy Policy

Garbage – Road to Ace
Effective date: October 7, 2026

Garbage – Road to Ace (“the Game”) is a single-player card game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the iOS and Android versions.

1. INFORMATION STORED BY THE GAME

The Game stores gameplay information locally on your device, including progress, points, unlocked and equipped cosmetics, achievements, game settings, audio settings, and saved Road progress. This local game data is used only to operate the Game and restore your progress.

The Game does not currently require an account, name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, camera access, microphone access, or payment-card information. The developer does not operate a server that receives your locally saved game progress.

Deleting the Game may delete locally stored progress, subject to your device platform’s backup and restore behavior.

2. iOS VERSION

The planned iOS edition is paid and ad-free. The Game itself does not send the locally stored gameplay profile to the developer.

Apple may independently process information related to downloading, purchasing, operating, diagnosing, or backing up apps under Apple’s own terms and privacy policies. That processing is controlled by Apple, not by the Game.

3. ANDROID VERSION AND GOOGLE ADMOB

The Android edition may display advertising using Google AdMob / the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising technology may automatically collect or receive information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID or app set ID when available;
  • app and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad/video views; and
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as crash or performance data.

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, ad measurement, security, and fraud prevention, subject to Google’s policies and the user’s available consent and device settings.

The developer does not receive your payment-card details from Google Play. If a Remove Ads purchase is offered, Google Play processes the transaction. The Game may store a local entitlement indicating that ads were removed.

4. PERSONALIZED ADS AND CONSENT

Where required by law or platform policy, the Android version will use the applicable Google consent tools before requesting or using information for personalized advertising. Available advertising choices may depend on your region, age eligibility, device settings, and Google settings.

5. SHARING OF INFORMATION

The developer does not sell your locally stored gameplay progress or personal information.

For the Android ad-supported version, information described above may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as necessary to provide advertising, analytics, measurement, security, and fraud-prevention services.

6. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Local gameplay data remains on the device until it is overwritten, reset by a game feature, removed through device/app data controls, or deleted when the Game is uninstalled, subject to platform backup behavior.

Because the Game currently has no developer-hosted user accounts or cloud profile database, the developer generally does not possess a server-side gameplay profile to delete.

Advertising data processed by Google is retained and deleted according to Google’s applicable policies and user controls.

7. CHILDREN

The Game is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising configuration and store age/content settings will be maintained in accordance with applicable platform requirements. If the Game is later directed to children or materially changes its data practices, this policy and the advertising configuration will be updated before release.

8. SECURITY

The Game minimizes developer-side data collection by keeping gameplay progress on the device. Third-party services used by the Android version are responsible for protecting information they process under their own security practices and policies.

9. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Depending on platform and release configuration, the Game may rely on:

  • Apple App Store / Apple services;
  • Google Play services; and
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK on Android.

Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Game adds or changes features, advertising, analytics, purchases, cloud services, or other data practices. The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are made.
